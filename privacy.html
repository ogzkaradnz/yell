Privacy Policy

Yell respects your privacy.

Yell is designed to help you show large, readable messages on your screen. We do not require an account and we do not directly collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.

Effective date: 1 January 2026

Data we collect

We do not directly collect personal data through Yell.

This means:

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal information is collected by us, such as your name, photos, contacts, or precise location.
  • No usage analytics are used by us.
  • No crash reporting services are used by us.
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies are used by us inside the app.

Local processing

Your messages and saved text items are processed on your device. The core app functionality does not require sending your message content to our servers.

Messages you create in the app are intended to remain on your device, unless your device settings or Apple services, such as iCloud backup, store app data as part of your normal device backup settings.

Third-party services

Yell does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services in the app.

The app may be distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple may process certain information related to downloads, purchases, app updates, diagnostics, or device settings according to Apple’s own privacy practices.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties. We do not store user personal data on our own servers.

Data retention

Because we do not directly collect personal data, we do not retain personal data on our servers.

Any text you save inside the app remains under your control on your device. You can remove your saved messages by deleting them in the app or deleting the app from your device.

Children’s privacy

Yell is a general utility app and is not intended to collect information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your privacy choices

You can manage device-level privacy and backup settings through iOS Settings. You can also delete the app at any time to remove locally stored app data from your device.

Because Yell is built around local, simple message display, your text content does not need to leave your device for the app’s main features to work.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the App Store listing.
